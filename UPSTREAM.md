# Upstream and provenance

Fieldbook is an independently maintained downstream fork of
[OldJobobo's Field Notes](https://github.com/OldJobobo/field-notes), used under the MIT
License. The Fieldbook branch starts from upstream commit
`2a0a5e97b96e1e76d784b0e90121c178e2fed2ba`.

The original copyright notice and the full MIT license remain in [LICENSE](LICENSE).
Fieldbook does not claim to be an official Field Notes release or to be endorsed by its
author.

Fieldbook retains the original drawing, text, undo, autosave, export, layer-shell and input
work, then adds its own product identity and organization layer. Its main additions include:

- chapters and stable page organization;
- sticky notes and pasted images;
- desktop pinning with page navigation;
- three page sizes and board placement per monitor;
- an Omarchy-themed toolbar and settings panel;
- stricter state validation, limits and recovery behavior.

When bringing upstream changes into Fieldbook, preserve OldJobobo's copyright notice and
record the upstream commit in the merge or commit message.
